Ten discs.That is the string a 66 kV line carries, and it is what the sub-station at Chitradurga needed. A house in Jayanagar needs one. This firm has wired both ends of that range.

Seventy-three jobs, measured in discs.

Every installation the firm has recorded, with the architect and electrical consultant of record and the load delivered. A disc insulator stack encodes voltage class — so each job below carries the stack its supply needed. Fifty-nine of the seventy-three state a connected load; the rest are fit-outs and layout works, recorded by area. Private residences are listed by locality only.

Everything here is porcelain, zinc, or copper.

Suchetana Electricals is a proprietorship, incorporated in 1969 by the late Shri Subramanyappa M., a licensed electrical engineer from Mysore State, and Class I from the outset. Fifty-seven years in continuous practice, under the same family and the same licence class. The firm works alongside civil construction, EPC and turnkey contractors, with the architect and electrical consultant of record on every job.

What we take on

  • HT & LT power supply — sanction through to commissioning
  • Distribution transformers, 44 to 1000 KVA
  • Sub-stations at 66/11 KV and 110/33 KV
  • HT cubicles, VCB panels, LBS and ring main units
  • HT cable laying, runs to 500 metres

And the rest of it

  • Single-pole, double-pole and spun-pole structures
  • 11 KV overhead line erection and street lighting
  • Interior electrification and workstation distribution
  • Layout electrification for villas and apartments
  • Rate analysis, quantity take-off and billing

Our first responsibility is to the customers who use our services.
