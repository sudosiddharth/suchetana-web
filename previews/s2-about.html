The firm

From sanctioned supply to the moment it goes live.

Suchetana Electricals is a proprietorship firm, incorporated in 1969 by the late Shri Subramanyappa M. — a licensed electrical engineer from Mysore State — and Class I from the outset. Fifty-seven years in continuous practice under the same family and the same licence class. The firm works alongside civil construction, EPC and turnkey contractors, and with the architect and electrical consultant of record on every job.
